What we heard

The brief

A minimal self-hosted image host for people who run their own VPS: upload publicly, share by link, resize on the fly, and never run a database. The mark has to look at home in a README, a terminal and a browser tab.

minimalprecisefriendlylighttrustworthy

Success: reads as a P at 16 px, survives one colour and reversing, avoids camera/cloud/upload-arrow clichés, and sits comfortably next to developer tooling.

Concept A

P monogram

The initial P whose counter is a square pixel: the letter and the idea in one shape.

  • Strongest at 16 px, so the favicon and the app icon carry the identity
  • The pixel counter ties it to an image service without drawing a camera
  • Built on a 40-unit grid: 30-unit stem, 40-unit counter, no curves to wobble
